Assessment & grading

Fair, structured, and practical

To be awarded the diploma, students must achieve a minimum of 50% overall, maintain at least 75% attendance, and successfully clear both theoretical and practical assessments.

  • Class participation, attendance & monthly practical performance
  • Theory examination across departments
  • Viva voce evaluating communication & subject knowledge

Examinations are conducted in affiliation with the Council for Vocational Education & Skill Development (CVESD), a statutory body that sets curriculum and qualification standards for vocational training across India. Graduates who clear the assessment receive a CVESD-recognised certificate.
